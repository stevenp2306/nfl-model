How it works

Every morning at 11:00 ET the model pulls standings, run scoring, probable starters and three days of bullpen usage from MLB's own data, the forecast at first pitch for open-air parks, and DraftKings and FanDuel prices, then projects runs for both teams in every game.

Edges. For sides, the edge is the model's win chance minus the market's after the vig is removed — 5 points to flag, 8 for 🔥 STRONG. Totals need 1.0 runs (1.5 for STRONG). Strikeout props need a 6-point edge and are capped at one unit.

What goes into a game. Starters are rated on a blend of FIP, ERA and their last five starts. Each lineup is adjusted for how it hits lefties or righties, every park uses Statcast's three-year run factor, bullpens are docked for recent workload, and the forecast at first pitch moves the total.

Quality gate. An edge alone isn't a bet. Sides and totals need both starters announced with real innings behind them, a play is dropped if the line is moving against it, and only the best 5 make the card each day. Anything skipped is explained under Why.

Units. ✅ SOLID 1u, 🔥 STRONG 2u, 💎 MAX 3u (11%+ on a side). A side only goes above 1u when the team is both good and hot; totals top out at 2u and strikeout props at 1u. Favorites are bet to win the units, underdogs risk them.

Honesty. This model is new. The record, beat-the-close rate and the calibration chart are the real test, and MLB grading is kept completely separate from football.
