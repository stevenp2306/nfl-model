How it works

Every team is rated on expected goals for and against per game (shot quality, adjusted for score and venue), with last season pulled toward average and this season taking over as games are played. Each likely goalie is rated on goals saved above expected. Tired teams on a back-to-back are docked.

Pricing. Goals are modelled as Poisson for 60 minutes, then empty-net goals are added when a team leads late, and tied games go to overtime or a shootout. That full score grid prices the moneyline, the puck line and every total.

Flags. Edges are the model's chance minus the market's after the vig is removed. Moneyline: 5% SOLID, 8% STRONG, 11% MAX. Puck line and totals need 5 / 8 / 11. Only the best 6 make the card each day.

Units. Every NHL bet is 1u for now: in last season's backtest, sizing up on bigger edges lost money, so tiers are shown but not sized. That changes once STRONG and MAX prove they win more.

Proven rules. Tracking only right now. The best rule from the first half of last season (totals (under only)) did not hold up on the second half, so no bets are recommended. The model keeps pricing every game, shows its leans, and gets re-tested as this season's results come in.

Timing. Each game is checked on its own. Every 15 minutes the model reads the starting goalies from DailyFaceoff, ESPN and Rotowire, and once both are confirmed it locks that game's bet at the current price. If a goalie is never confirmed, that game simply gets no bet. The goalie is the single biggest input, so the model won't guess.

Honesty. Brand new. The record and the calibration chart are the real test, and NHL grading is kept completely separate.
