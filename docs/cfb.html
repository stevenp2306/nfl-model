How it works

Every FBS team has a power rating — points better or worse than an average team on a neutral field — and a total lean. The model spread is the rating difference plus 2.5 for home field; the model total is a base plus both teams' leans. Ratings move 25% of each miss after every game.

The card. Every game is priced, but only the 8 strongest plays each week become bets — MAX first, then STRONG, then SOLID, biggest edge first. Picks lock on game day. Everything else with an edge shows as "watch" on the slate.

Tiers. Same as NFL and MLB: ✅ SOLID at 8 points of disagreement with the book, 🔥 STRONG at 11, 💎 MAX at 14. College lines are softer and noisier than the NFL, so the bar is higher.

Units. ✅ SOLID 1u, 🔥 STRONG 2u, 💎 MAX 3u, bet to win at -110. The whole record is stated on that scale.

Honesty. The Model vs the market tiles are the real test: if the book keeps landing closer to the final margin, the edges aren't real yet.
